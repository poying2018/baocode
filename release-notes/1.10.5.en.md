BaoCode Claude 1.10.5

The upstream's work through 1.9.6: group chats for bots, editing a sent
message can put files back, BaoCode Bash on Windows, and many fixes.

New
- Group chats: two or more bots in one chat, taking turns; each keeps what
  it heard and said in its own files, and can forget it.
- Sending an earlier message again can also put back what the later turns
  did to the files (Settings → General → Edited Messages).
- BaoCode Bash: a bundled bash with rg, grep, sed, awk, find and jq, for
  Windows' terminals and the commands agents run.
- Up to 100 pictures in one message.
- A project's folder can have folders added to it, as a workspace.
- Claude subscription limits show when they reset.
- The phone can upload files here, and a push says what an agent did while
  you were away; the About page shows this computer's ID.
- Settings can be moved to another computer; GitCode accounts added.

Fixed
- The tray icon comes back when Explorer restarts.
- Formulas in a table's cells show typeset without clicking in.
- Dragging a window raises it; links to folders open again.
- Archived agents are grouped under their folder.
