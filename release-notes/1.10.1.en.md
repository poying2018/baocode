BaoCode Claude 1.10.1

The workbench's panes, cards and terminals as upstream has them, with the
commands and fixes that 1.10.0 left out.

New
- Panes have the row along their top: each one's tabs, its project and its
  close button on its card, and Open in Editor once for all of them.
- The terminal's and the side panel's buttons are on each conversation's
  card, on Windows too, and the panel is under its own conversation.
- Go Back and Go Forward beside the title bar's search, in the IDE as in the
  window's header (#539); Add Selection to Chat (Ctrl+L / Cmd+L) reaches the
  composer again.
- A conversation gone on with elsewhere can be read anew in place.

Fixed
- /btw and /fork reach the conversations they open again.
- An image's diff reads its bytes through the project's files, a remote
  project's too.
- A markdown drop or a draft's code names its file the project's way: a POSIX
  project keeps its slashes on Windows.
- Search results follow an open file's unsaved edits and a file changed on
  disk.
- A host's projects come back by what went off with it, across restarts too.
- MCP probes size every request, so servers that read only by Content-Length
  answer; the download pages list the Android app again.
