Markdown, edited where it shows

The stashes, a markdown file's preview and the chat's input are now one editor
of markdown: it shows the text as markdown, the marks it carries are the ones
the caret is in, and it is edited where it is written rather than in a
separate box.

New
- One markdown editor for the stashes, a markdown file's preview and the chat's input: the text is shown as markdown, and the formatting bar follows the caret, offering the marks that make sense there.
- A markdown file's preview is edited in place: the text under the caret changes as you type, with no separate editing window.
- The stashes keep their formatting bar in a preview too, in code as well: only the proofreading marks are offered there.
- The formatting bar follows the selection as the text scrolls, and goes away while the selection is out of view. A whole styling of the text is one Undo.
- Tables are edited in their cells: rows and columns get handles and menus of their own, bars add them, a cell's text is picked from the first press, and cells dragged across are picked as a spreadsheet's (copied, cut, cleared, pasted). The outline follows the lines and is round at the table's corners, and a wide table gets a scrollbar while the mouse is over it.
- Links show as they do when clicked, with a bubble over them to open, copy, edit or remove them; a link opened with the keyboard's command key stays where it went. A right click has the clipboard's, the Undo's, the link's and the selection's marks.
- Inline code's ground is painted under the selection, an emptied line keeps its height, and the caret is as high as its line.

Improved
- The editor of the chat's input is quicker to draw: it no longer redraws what the text behind the caret does not change.
- A selection made across lines keeps its marks as the text is edited around it.
